# Aiden's Way Of Japanese (README):
This website is a documentation of the way I learned Japanese. My journey, hence the name.
I have researched, put in my hours, and tried many different techniques to learning.

This website is meant to help anyone who wishes to learn Japanese too, but it's also just documentation of what I did.

## Who Is This For?

This is REALLY important. Not everyone has the same style of learning, that's why you should only dedicate your time to learning Japanese with this website as a tool if you fit the description below.

### Work Ethic:
Willing to challenge yourself, to push yourself past some limits. You may think you've done enough today, but a lot of times that's the point where you've done *almost* enough. You're going to have to consume more content (studying, immersion, etc) than you may be comfortable with because it will *genuinely* make things stick better.

This is variable though. For example, I'm making this website while I'm studying Japanese while I am in school. I do Cross Country, study geometry, and am aiming for Wharton. I don't have time to fit in 2 hours of Japanese on the side, but I can do 50 minutes, feel like it's enough, and then do an hour an 10 minutes.

### Type Of Learner:
I mean like visual, auditory, or manual...

**manual**

I have texts and videos so my website offers a little bit of everything, but not *everything*. This means that you will have to do some of your own work/researching/studying.

## Something Important:
### This Website Is A *Supplement*,
Meaning that if you do use this to study Japanese, it's meant to be used as a tool to help you along your own journey. I don't want to tell you how to learn Japanese, but if you find one of my ways of mastering something by all means do it. You should also do some of your own research to get a re-wording and enforcement of what you just learned.
