Alphabets:

Master Hiragana and Katakana before studying any other Japanese.

Hiragana

(3 Mastering Techniques)

  1. To master the characters, the first step is to engrave them into your mind, even a little. You do this by taking it one row at a time. For example, write one row of Hiragana down (あいうえお). Look at each character and try to draw it in your mind or pull up a picture of it. Once you've done that once, walk away and start conjuring up the characters. In order to see real progress you must push yourself, this means doing three rows at a time. Trust me, it actually makes mastering it faster when you do a lot. Anways, you just repeat the process for all rows.
  • Reading simple Japanese texts is really effective for helping your reading speed and consistency. This is highly recommended before moving on because this will help engrave Hiragana into your mind and help you when you do start stuyding more Japanese. I have left links below to websites you can read texts at. (oh, and also just to make sure you know, the reading is not meant to be understood)
